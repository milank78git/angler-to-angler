Account and optional online features · pilot 0.11.0

When registering by email, you choose a password. The language of the app at registration determines your account email language. Email verification and password recovery use a single-use link. A secured phone can store an encrypted credential for subsequent unlocks. Android verifies your screen lock or biometrics. Signing out removes the credential from this phone.

Saving a profile stores your chosen picture, hometown, tagline and biography with your pilot account. The card is initially private. If you publish it, signed-in pilot anglers can see the card and photo-documented catch total. They see only groups they share with you. You can remove the picture, clear the fields and withdraw publication under Edit profile.

You choose each catch photo to send for review. The reviewer receives a resized photo without original metadata, species, capture time, water name and approximate location rounded to 0.01 degrees. The hosted image, water and location are removed after a decision. A photo may itself reveal the fishing spot. The pilot retains the result, length, capture and decision times, identifiers and image fingerprint until you remove the review record or close the account. Pending photos stay in the review queue until a reviewer decides, you withdraw them or the account is deleted. You can withdraw a record under Profile → My submitted catch photos. Deleting a local trip removes its local copy. Backup retention and contact details are in the deployment notice.

When you post a trip tip to a group, current members see your display name, posting time and the exact text in the preview. Your tips are deleted when you leave the group, the group is dissolved or your account is erased. Deleting the local trip only deletes its local copy.

Questions and answers at a water are visible to verified pilot anglers. Posting shares your display name, text, publication time and the trip date you enter for an answer. You can withdraw your text until someone posts a public text reply to it. If a moderator removes a post or its author erases their account, other anglers’ replies remain in the thread. Reports go privately to the pilot operator. Account erasure removes your contributions and reports.

You can reply privately to the author of a water question. Both participants can see the text, display name and posting time in the app. Blocking stops new messages between the two accounts across all waters. History stays readable. The recipient can report a particular message for operator review. Erasing either account removes the shared conversation. Each participant can add, replace or remove one emoji reaction per message. During a block you can still remove your own reaction.

You enter a catch photo into a group of your choice. Members see your display name, species, trip date, resized photo without metadata, vote count and rank. The surroundings in the photo may reveal the spot. Each member can cast or remove one vote per other catch in the group. Rounds use the Prague calendar week. You may withdraw an entry after voting closes. Deleting the local trip does not withdraw the shared entry. Leaving the group removes your entries and votes in that group.

You can choose to contribute or update each completed trip, including a blank trip. Your account stores the trip identifier, water, calendar month, target species, main fishing method and total number of recorded fish. Other signed-in anglers see aggregate results only. Deleting a local trip does not withdraw the contribution. You can withdraw it later under Profile → My activity contributions. Erasing your account removes all your contributions.
